Features

Power of an AI agent, discipline of a vault

Voice assistant

Speech-to-text and natural voice replies. Short, clear confirmations instead of raw hashes.

Policy Engine

Daily and per-trade limits, slippage caps, price-impact guard and trusted-contract reputation.

SandboxGuard

Local pre-flight simulation of every Web3 transaction before you sign.

Safe Scan & Panic

Finds every active token approval and lets you revoke risky ones in one place.

dApp browser

HTTPS only, the real domain always visible, separate consent for each site, readable previews with fees in coin and USD.

Exchanges

Spot trading on MEXC, OKX, Gate.io and Binance through encrypted API keys. Keys with withdrawal permission are rejected.

Non-custodial wallet

History you can read

Native and token transfers with sender, recipient, amount, status, time, hash and fee.

Eternal Permission Kernel

A power of attorney your AI cannot exceed

An AI that acts for you needs a mandate: what it may do, how much, where and until when. EPK writes that mandate as on-chain rules for your smart account — verifiable by anyone, enforced every time.

  • Per-transaction and daily spend limits
  • Allowed targets and functions only
  • Threat-feed blocklists
  • Instant revoke with one panic switch
Live on BSC Testnet · mainnet after audit
